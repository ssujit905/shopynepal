Privacy Policy

Updated: September 2026

Shopy Nepal (Kathmandu, Nepal) collects only the data needed to process your orders and improve your shopping experience. Contact: support@shopynepal.com.

1. Data we collect

Name, phone, delivery address, email, order history, and support messages. We also store anonymized page-view and search analytics to improve the store.

2. How we use it

To confirm, deliver and support orders, process COD / eSewa / FonePay payments, prevent fraud, and send order SMS updates. We never sell your data.

3. Payments

Card / wallet details are handled by eSewa and FonePay on their pages. We only receive the payment status, never your PIN or full credentials.

4. Cookies & analytics

We use a session id and cached preferences (cart, recently viewed) in your browser. Page visits are logged in anonymized form. No third-party ad trackers.

5. Data sharing

Shared only with delivery partners (name, phone, address) and payment gateways (amount, order id). Staff access is restricted by role.

6. Retention & deletion

Order records are kept for accounting and warranty purposes. To request export or deletion of your account data, email support@shopynepal.com — we respond within 7 days.
